Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

How accurate is the AI food recognition? +
CalorieCam AI is powered by Google's state-of-the-art Gemini multimodal vision models, trained on millions of culinary dishes worldwide. It achieves over 95% accuracy on common meals, and you can always adjust detected portions before saving.
Is CalorieCam AI free to use? +
Yes! Every user receives 15 free AI analysis tokens immediately upon signup. You can also log manual meals, water, and steps completely free forever. Extra AI tokens can be earned by watching rewarded videos or purchased in affordable packs.
Can I use the app without creating an account? +
Absolutely. We offer a dedicated "Continue as Guest" mode. In Guest mode, all your meal records, macros, and history are stored strictly locally on your Android device's SQLite database without uploading any personal email.
Are my photos kept private and secure? +
Yes. Photos are processed exclusively over encrypted TLS connections for the sole purpose of identifying food and computing nutritional information. We never sell, share, or use your photos for advertising. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Which Android versions are supported? +
CalorieCam AI supports Android 7.0 (Nougat, API 24) all the way up to Android 15 / 16 (API 35+), ensuring seamless compatibility across virtually all modern Android smartphones and tablets.
